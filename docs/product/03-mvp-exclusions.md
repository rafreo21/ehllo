# ehllo MVP Exclusions

Last updated: 2026-07-24

These exclusions protect the initial learning goal: determine whether independent consultants will repeatedly capture encounter context and complete better follow-ups.

## Excluded initial audiences

The MVP will not be optimised for:

- Enterprise sales teams
- Conference exhibitors
- Recruiters
- Investors
- Large agencies
- Students or casual networkers
- Personal or dating relationships
- Field-sales organisations
- Customer-success departments
- Companies seeking a complete CRM replacement

## Excluded features

The MVP will not initially include:

- Native iOS or Android applications
- NFC card manufacturing or fulfilment
- Team workspaces, roles, permissions, or administration
- Enterprise SSO
- CRM replacement functionality
- Automated contact enrichment
- Lead scoring
- Event lead-routing workflows
- Conference exhibitor analytics
- Ambient or passive meeting recording
- Autonomous message sending
- Unreviewed AI actions
- Multi-step marketing campaigns
- Shared corporate address books
- Organisation-wide templates
- Advanced attribution
- Custom workflow automation
- Full email or calendar replacement

## Deliberately limited MVP capabilities

- One individual workspace
- One primary public professional identity
- Account-free recipient experience
- User-initiated encounter capture
- User-reviewed context extraction
- User-reviewed follow-up drafting
- Email or calendar handoff rather than replacement
- A focused follow-up queue centred on completed actions

## Revisit rule

An excluded audience or feature should be reconsidered only when validated individual usage demonstrates retention and the proposed expansion supports the encounter-to-follow-up outcome without obscuring the core product.
