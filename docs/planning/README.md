# ehllo

Research and product planning for a meeting-to-follow-up assistant inspired by the problem Blinq solves-not a copy of its brand or implementation.

## Recommendation

Build a focused **meeting-to-follow-up assistant for independent professionals and small relationship-driven teams**.

The product begins with a shareable digital identity card, but its main job is to turn each real-world meeting into:

1. a clean contact,
2. remembered context,
3. a suggested follow-up,
4. and a trackable relationship.

The initial target could be narrowed to one of:

- real-estate agents,
- recruiters,
- consultants and fractional executives,
- conference exhibitors,
- or local service-business owners.

The best starting segment is probably **independent consultants and small agencies**. They have frequent high-value conversations, use lightweight CRMs poorly or not at all, and can adopt without enterprise procurement.

## Working product concept

Temporary name: **ehllo**

Positioning:

> Remember every person. Make the next move.

Core loop:

`Share or scan → capture context → create follow-up → strengthen relationship`

This should be validated before committing to a final name, visual identity, native apps, NFC hardware, or enterprise integrations.
