# What we could build

## Option 1: Vertical digital identity

Build digital profiles, QR sharing, lead capture, and follow-up for one profession.

Examples:

- Realtors: listings, service areas, brokerage compliance, buyer/seller intent, viewing follow-up.
- Recruiters: open roles, candidate/client mode, CV capture, consent, ATS handoff.
- Consultants: expertise, case studies, availability, discovery notes, proposal follow-up.

**Advantages:** Clear marketing, opinionated workflows, easier early sales.

**Weakness:** The segment must be large and painful enough to overcome established horizontal products.

**Verdict:** Strong if we already have distribution in a profession.

## Option 2: Meeting-to-follow-up assistant - recommended

Build a personal relationship workspace where a card or scanner starts the workflow, but follow-up is the product.

Core behavior:

- Share a public profile through a link or QR code.
- Let the other person return their information without registering.
- Add a quick typed or dictated post-meeting note.
- Extract context, commitments, topics, and a follow-up date.
- Draft a personal follow-up message.
- Maintain a private relationship timeline.
- Provide a daily “people to follow up with” queue.

**Advantages:** Focuses on a valuable job Blinq supports but does not exclusively own; useful without hardware or enterprise integrations; can become a lightweight relationship CRM.

**Weakness:** Follow-up tools need exceptionally low friction or users stop logging meetings.

**Verdict:** Best balance of differentiation, buildability, and expansion potential.

## Option 3: Event lead-capture system

Build a mobile scanning and qualification app for exhibitors, with instant routing and event ROI.

Core behavior:

- Scan badge QR codes and business cards.
- Configure qualification questions.
- Attach typed or dictated notes.
- Deduplicate and enrich leads.
- Send them to HubSpot or Salesforce.
- Track response time, meetings, opportunities, and event ROI.

**Advantages:** Clear business ROI and larger budgets.

**Weakness:** Crowded enterprise market; badge formats and organizer APIs vary; offline mobile reliability and CRM depth are demanding.

**Verdict:** Attractive second product or a first product only with an event-industry partner.

## Decision

Start with Option 2 and design it so one vertical can be layered on later.

Do not begin with:

- custom NFC card manufacturing,
- ambient conversation recording,
- native iOS and Android applications,
- 20 CRM integrations,
- enterprise identity provisioning,
- event badge API partnerships,
- or a full contact-enrichment data operation.

Those are expansion features after the core loop proves that users return and complete follow-ups.
